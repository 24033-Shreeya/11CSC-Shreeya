About us

The Last Straw is a non-profit organisation dedicated to protecting our oceans from plastic pollution. We raise awareness, organise beach clean-ups, and encourage people to make environmentally friendly choices.

Our mission is to create cleaner, healthier oceans for marine life and future generations. We believe that every small action can make a big difference.

Together, we can reduce pollution and protect our oceans.

Don’t let plastic become the last straw. Act now!

Our Kaupapa

Our kaupapa is to protect our moana by reducing plastic pollution and encouraging people to care for the environment. We believe everyone has a role to play in keeping our oceans clean and protecting marine life.

Through education, community action, and sustainable choices, we aim to inspire positive change. By working together, we can create a cleaner, healthier future for our oceans and for the generations to come.
